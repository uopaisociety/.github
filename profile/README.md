# AI Society

**University of Portsmouth**

University of Portsmouth's student-led AI community. Beginners welcome. Learn, build and connect through workshops, projects and events.

We're a free UPSU society, being rebuilt for 2026/27. Students from any course can join.

## What we're about

- **Workshops** — Hands-on sessions you can follow as a beginner.
- **Projects** — Build and experiment with other students.
- **Events** — Talks, meetings and chances to meet people interested in AI.
